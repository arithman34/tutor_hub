TutorHub is a tool an independent tutor uses to manage their own tutoring business. Accounts are created by invitation. This policy explains what TutorHub stores, what it does with Google account data, and how to remove it.

What TutorHub stores

  • Your account: name, email address, postal address (optional) and a hashed password.
  • Records you enter: students, payees, lesson sessions and their notes, and payments.
  • A daily count of TutorHub AI questions, used to enforce a usage limit.
  • If you connect Google: the OAuth tokens needed to reach your calendar, and the ID of the calendar TutorHub created.
  • If you connect Zoom: the OAuth tokens needed to read your meetings and their summaries, and a record of each lesson meeting TutorHub has logged.

Data is stored in an encrypted database on Amazon Web Services in London (eu-west-2), with encrypted backups. Traffic reaches TutorHub over HTTPS through Cloudflare. Each account can only see its own records.

Google account data

If you connect a Google account, TutorHub asks for one permission, Calendar (calendar.app.created). It lets TutorHub create one secondary calendar called "Tutoring", and create and read events on that calendar only. TutorHub cannot see or change your other calendars, and does not access Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

Events are read from Google when you open the sessions page and are not stored. If you log a lesson from a calendar event, that lesson becomes one of your TutorHub records.

TutorHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide TutorHub's calendar features to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, not sent to AI services, and not used to train AI models. No person reads it except where you ask for help, for security purposes, or where the law requires.

Zoom account data

If you connect a Zoom account, TutorHub asks for three read-only permissions: view a past meeting's instances (meeting:read:list_past_instances), view a past meeting (meeting:read:past_meeting) and view a meeting's summary (meeting:read:summary). TutorHub cannot start, change or delete meetings, and does not access recordings, transcripts, chat or contacts.

When one of your students' meetings ends and its AI Companion summary is ready, Zoom notifies TutorHub. TutorHub then reads that meeting's start and end times, how many people attended (to tell a lesson from a no-show) and its summary, and logs the lesson: the summary is sent to OpenAI to fill in the lesson notes (see below), and the summary, notes and meeting ID are stored with the lesson. TutorHub also checks your students' past meetings from the last week when it starts up or when you ask it to, to catch any lessons it missed. Only meetings whose ID matches one of your students are read. You can also fetch a lesson's summary yourself with Fetch from Zoom.

AI features

TutorHub AI and Zoom summary parsing use the OpenAI API. When you use them, your question, the relevant TutorHub records it looks up (such as student names, sessions and payments), or the Zoom summary you paste, fetch or TutorHub receives from Zoom are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. Google account data is never sent. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models.

Cookies

TutorHub sets only the cookies it needs to work: sign-in cookies, a CSRF protection cookie, and a session cookie that holds your current TutorHub AI conversation. Your light or dark mode choice is kept in your browser's local storage. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Your choices

  • Export: download all your records as a JSON file from the My Data page.
  • Disconnect Google: use the Connections page to delete TutorHub's Google tokens. You can also remove TutorHub's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The "Tutoring" calendar stays in your Google account until you delete it.
  • Disconnect Zoom: use the Connections page. TutorHub revokes its access with Zoom and deletes its Zoom tokens. You can also remove TutorHub from the Manage Apps page of the Zoom App Marketplace.
  • Delete your account: ask using the contact below, and your account and all its records will be deleted.
